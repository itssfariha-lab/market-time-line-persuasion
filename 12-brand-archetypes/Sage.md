# Sage

## 1. Definition

The Sage archetype represents the search for truth and understanding. Sage brands help people learn, think carefully, and make informed decisions.

## 2. Characteristics

- Values knowledge and clear thinking.
- Shares information and expertise.
- Seeks accuracy and understanding.
- Encourages thoughtful decisions.

## 3. Imagery
![Sage archetype](../images/sage.jpeg)
Books, libraries, teachers, research, or clear diagrams can represent the Sage. These images connect the archetype with learning, evidence, and the sharing of knowledge.

## 4. Color Scheme

Blue: Suggests trust and clear communication.

White: Can make information feel clear and organized.

Green: Can suggest learning and steady growth.

These are suggested design choices, not fixed colors for the archetype.

## 5. Examples

Google is often associated with the Sage because its search tools help people find information and answers.

## 6. References

Mark, Margaret, and Carol S. Pearson. *The Hero and the Outlaw: Building Extraordinary Brands Through the Power of Archetypes*. McGraw-Hill, 2001. ISBN 978-0-07-136415-7. This book presents the brand archetype framework. The Google association is an illustrative interpretation.
