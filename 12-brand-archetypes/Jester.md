# Jester

## 1. Definition

The Jester archetype represents humor, playfulness, and enjoying the present moment. Jester brands use fun to make experiences feel lively and less serious.

## 2. Characteristics

- Uses humor and play.
- Has a lively and lighthearted personality.
- Encourages people to enjoy the moment.
- Often surprises people with wit or silliness.

## 3. Imagery
![Jester archetype](../images/Jester.jpeg)
Bright scenes, playful characters, jokes, or people laughing can represent the Jester. These visuals help communicate fun, humor, and spontaneity.

## 4. Color Scheme

Yellow: Can suggest cheerfulness and energy.

Orange: Adds warmth and a playful feeling.

Red: Can make a design feel lively and attention-grabbing.

These are suggested design choices, not fixed colors for the archetype.

## 5. Examples

M&M's is often associated with the Jester because its colorful characters and advertising use humor and playful situations.

## 6. References

Mark, Margaret, and Carol S. Pearson. *The Hero and the Outlaw: Building Extraordinary Brands Through the Power of Archetypes*. McGraw-Hill, 2001. ISBN 978-0-07-136415-7. This book presents the brand archetype framework. The M&M's association is an illustrative interpretation.
