## About Me

I am a student at the New Jersey Institute of Technology studying Business Information Systems. I am interested in technology, business, web development, and design. Through this project, I have been able to explore different design movements, brand archetypes, and methods of persuasion while also gaining more experience working with GitHub.

## My Issues and Contributions

I worked primarily on researching Modernist and Postmodernist design movements. I researched six Modernist and six Postmodernist movements and explored what each style represents, including their characteristics, imagery, color schemes, examples, and references.

I also worked on the Brand Archetypes and Methods of Persuasion section. I connected the Jester, Magician, Lover, and Ruler archetypes with different persuasion methods and added explanations, real-world examples, and imagery.

- **Modernism & Postmodernism Design Styles** — Researched six Modernist and six Postmodernist design movements, including their characteristics, imagery, color schemes, examples, and references.
- **Archetypes & Methods of Persuasion** — Connected the Jester, Magician, Lover, and Ruler archetypes with persuasion principles and added examples and imagery.
- **Project Images & References** — Added and organized images and sources for the design movement and archetype sections.
