# Radical Design

## 1. Definition

Radical Design was an experimental design movement that developed in Italy during the 1960s and 1970s. It challenged traditional ideas about architecture, furniture, and design by creating unusual and provocative objects that questioned how design should look and function.

## 2. Characteristics

- Experimental and unconventional designs
- Unusual shapes and forms
- Rejection of traditional design rules
- Playful and provocative ideas
- Influence from popular culture
- Focus on creativity and social commentary

## 3. Imagery

Radical Design often features unusual furniture, exaggerated shapes, bright patterns, and experimental interiors. Many designs were intentionally strange or impractical in order to challenge traditional expectations.

## 4. Color Scheme

Radical Design does not have one specific color scheme. Designers often used bright and contrasting colors, bold patterns, and unexpected combinations to make their work stand out from traditional modernist design.

## 5. Examples

- Studio 65's Bocca sofa
- Archizoom Associati's experimental furniture and architecture
- Superstudio's conceptual architecture and design projects
