# Memphis Design

## 1. Definition

Memphis Design is a postmodern design movement that began in Milan, Italy, in the early 1980s. The movement rejected the simple and functional style of modernism and instead used bold colors, unusual shapes, patterns, and playful designs.

## 2. Characteristics

- Bright and bold colors
- Geometric and irregular shapes
- Playful and unusual patterns
- Asymmetrical designs
- Combination of different materials
- Designs that challenge traditional ideas of good taste

## 3. Imagery

Memphis Design often includes triangles, circles, squiggly lines, dots, and other geometric patterns. Furniture and graphics usually have an exaggerated, colorful, and playful appearance.

## 4. Color Scheme

Memphis Design commonly uses bright colors such as pink, yellow, blue, green, red, and turquoise. Black and white patterns are also frequently used to create strong contrast.

## 5. Examples

- Ettore Sottsass's Carlton room divider
- Furniture created by the Memphis Group
- Colorful geometric patterns used in 1980s graphic and interior design
