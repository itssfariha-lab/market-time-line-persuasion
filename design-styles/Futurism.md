# Futurism

## 1. Definition

Futurism was a modernist art and design movement that began in Italy in the early 20th century. It celebrated technology, speed, movement, machines, and the energy of modern life.

## 2. Characteristics

- Strong sense of movement and speed
- Dynamic and diagonal lines
- Repeated shapes and forms
- Experimental typography
- Geometric and mechanical imagery
- Focus on technology and modern life

## 3. Imagery

Futurist designs often show movement through repeated shapes, diagonal lines, and overlapping forms. Common imagery includes machines, automobiles, cities, and other symbols of technology and speed.

## 4. Color Scheme

Futurist artwork often uses bold and contrasting colors such as red, blue, yellow, orange, black, and white. Bright colors and strong contrasts help create a feeling of energy, motion, and excitement.

## 5. Examples

- Umberto Boccioni's *Unique Forms of Continuity in Space*
- Giacomo Balla's paintings showing movement and speed
- Futurist posters and experimental typography
