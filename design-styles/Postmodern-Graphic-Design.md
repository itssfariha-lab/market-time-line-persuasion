# Postmodern Graphic Design

## 1. Definition

Postmodern Graphic Design developed as a reaction against the strict rules and simplicity of modernist design. It became especially influential during the 1970s and 1980s and encouraged designers to experiment with different styles, typography, colors, and historical references.

## 2. Characteristics

- Experimental layouts
- Mixture of different design styles
- Bold and unusual typography
- Layered images and text
- Playful and expressive designs
- Rejection of strict modernist rules

## 3. Imagery

Postmodern Graphic Design often combines photographs, illustrations, patterns, shapes, and typography. Designs can appear busy or unconventional because different visual elements are intentionally mixed together.

## 4. Color Scheme

Postmodern Graphic Design does not have one specific color scheme. Designers often use bright, contrasting, or unexpected color combinations to make designs more expressive and visually interesting.

## 5. Examples

- April Greiman's graphic design
- Paula Scher's experimental typography
- 1980s posters and advertisements combining different visual styles
