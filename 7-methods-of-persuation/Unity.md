# Unity

## 1. Definition

Unity is a method of persuasion where people are more likely to be influenced by someone they consider part of their own group or community. It is based on shared identity, belonging, and the feeling of being connected to others.

## 2. Characteristics

- Builds influence through shared identity.
- Creates a sense of belonging and community.
- Encourages cooperation among group members.
- Uses shared values, experiences, and interests to build connections.

## 4. Color Scheme

Blue: Represents trust and unity.

Green: Represents harmony and connection.

These colors can be used in a design to communicate belonging and cooperation.

## 5. Examples

A university promotes its merchandise by encouraging students to show their school pride. Students may be more willing to purchase the merchandise because they feel connected to their university community.

Another example is a local business encouraging community members to support neighborhood stores by emphasizing their shared identity and values.
