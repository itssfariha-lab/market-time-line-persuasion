
# Authority

## 1. Definition

Authority is a method of persuasion where people are more likely to trust and follow recommendations from someone they believe has knowledge, experience, or expertise in a particular subject.

## 2. Characteristics

- Influences people through expertise and credibility.
- Uses professional knowledge and experience.
- Encourages people to trust expert recommendations.
- Can involve professionals, specialists, or respected leaders.

## 4. Color Scheme

Blue: Represents trust and professionalism.

White: Represents cleanliness and credibility.

These colors can be used in a design to communicate professionalism and trust.

## 5. Examples

A dentist recommends a particular toothpaste brand. Customers may be more willing to purchase it because they trust the dentist's professional knowledge.

Another example is a fitness trainer recommending exercise equipment based on their experience.
