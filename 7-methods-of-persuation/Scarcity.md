# Scarcity

## 1. Definition

Scarcity is a method of persuasion where people may value something more when it is limited or difficult to obtain. When a product is available for a short time or in a limited quantity, customers may feel more motivated to purchase it before it becomes unavailable.

## 2. Characteristics

- Creates a sense of urgency.
- Uses limited availability or limited-time offers.
- Can increase the perceived value of a product.
- Encourages people to make decisions before an opportunity disappears.

## 4. Color Scheme

Red: Represents urgency and excitement.

Black: Represents exclusivity and limited availability.

These colors can be used in a design to communicate urgency and exclusivity.

## 5. Examples

A clothing store releases a limited-edition collection with only 100 pieces available. Customers may feel motivated to purchase an item before it sells out.

Another example is an online store offering a discount for only 24 hours.
