# Commitment and Consistency

## 1. Definition

Commitment and Consistency is a method of persuasion where people are more likely to follow through with something after they have already made a decision or commitment. People often want their actions to match their previous choices and promises.

## 2. Characteristics

- Encourages people to follow through with their decisions.
- Works through small commitments that may lead to bigger ones.
- People often want their actions to match their beliefs and promises.
- Commitments made voluntarily can influence future decisions.

Blue: Represents trust, reliability, and commitment.

Green: Represents growth, progress, and consistency.

These colors can be used in a design to represent commitment and personal progress.

## 5. Examples

A gym offers a free trial to new customers. After signing up and attending a few classes, some customers may be more willing to purchase a full membership because they have already committed to exercising.

Another example is a person setting a goal to save money every month. After following the goal for several months, they may feel motivated to continue because they want to remain consistent with their original decision.
