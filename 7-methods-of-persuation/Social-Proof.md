# Social Proof (Consensus)

## 1. Definition

Social Proof is a method of persuasion where people look at the actions and opinions of others to help them make decisions. When many people support or choose something, others may feel more comfortable making the same choice.

## 2. Characteristics

- Influences people through the actions of others.
- Builds confidence through reviews and recommendations.
- Often works when people are unsure about a decision.
- Uses popularity and shared experiences to influence choices.
