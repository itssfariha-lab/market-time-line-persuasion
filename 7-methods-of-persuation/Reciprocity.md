# Reciprocity

## 1. Definition

Reciprocity is a method of persuasion where people feel the need to return a favor when someone does something nice for them. In marketing, businesses use this principle by offering something valuable to customers, hoping they will respond positively.

## 2. Characteristics

- Based on giving and receiving.
- Creates a feeling of appreciation.
- Encourages people to return favors.
- Can help build trust and relationships.

## 4. Color Scheme

Blue: Represents trust and reliability.

Green: Represents generosity and positive relationships.

These colors can be used in a visual design to communicate trust and generosity. They are suggested design choices, not fixed colors for reciprocity.

## 5. Examples

A grocery store offers free food samples to customers. After trying the product, some customers may feel more willing to purchase it.

Another example is a company offering a free trial of its service before asking customers to subscribe.
