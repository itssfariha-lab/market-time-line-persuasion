# Liking

## 1. Definition

Liking is a method of persuasion where people are more likely to be influenced by someone they like, admire, or feel connected to. People often trust recommendations from friends, family members, or individuals with similar interests.

## 2. Characteristics

- Builds influence through positive relationships.
- Uses friendliness, familiarity, and shared interests.
- Encourages trust through personal connections.
- People may be more receptive to recommendations from individuals they admire.

## 3. Imagery

An image of two friends smiling while one recommends a product to the other.

The image represents Liking because people may be more willing to consider recommendations from someone they know and trust.

## 4. Color Scheme

Pink: Represents friendliness and positive emotions.

Yellow: Represents happiness and warmth.

These colors can be used in a design to communicate friendliness and connection.

## 5. Examples

A customer purchases a skincare product because a close friend recommended it.

Another example is a person purchasing a clothing brand because their favorite influencer promotes it.
