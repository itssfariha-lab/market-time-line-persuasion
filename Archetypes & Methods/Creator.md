## Creator

### Matching Method of Persuasion
**Commitment & Consistency**

### Why They Match
The Creator archetype focuses on imagination, originality, and bringing new ideas to life. Commitment & Consistency matches because creators often build loyalty by encouraging people to stay involved in a creative process, project, or community over time.

### Real-World Example
LEGO fits the Creator archetype because it encourages people to build their own creations and continue creating with its products.
