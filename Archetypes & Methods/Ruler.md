##  Ruler

### Matching Methods of Persuasion
- **Authority**
- **Scarcity**
- **Social Proof**

### Why They Match
The Ruler archetype represents leadership, control, success, status, and high standards. **Authority** works because Ruler brands want to appear established and respected. **Scarcity** can create exclusivity and make a product feel prestigious. **Social Proof** can also work when successful or respected people use and support the brand.

### Real-World Examples
- **Rolex** – Represents prestige, achievement, luxury, and status.
- **Mercedes-Benz** – Represents luxury, success, quality, and high standards.

### Example of Persuasion
A luxury watch company could advertise a limited-edition watch that is worn by successful professionals.

This uses **Scarcity** because the watch is limited, **Social Proof** because successful people wear it, and **Authority** because the brand presents itself as established and prestigious.
