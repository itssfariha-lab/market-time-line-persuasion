## Every Person

### Matching Method of Persuasion
**Liking**

### Why They Match
The Every Person archetype focuses on being relatable, friendly, and creating a sense of belonging. Liking matches because people are more likely to be persuaded by people or brands they relate to, like, or feel connected with.

### Real-World Example
IKEA can represent the Every Person archetype because its branding focuses on practical, affordable products designed for ordinary everyday life.
